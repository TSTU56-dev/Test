From the roastery

We sell the product. We also run the ritual.

Hearthlane started as a two-kilo drum in a shared kitchen. The beans still come off that same curve: light enough to taste origin, dark enough to hold milk. The service side grew because shops and offices kept asking us to stop dropping bags and start owning the bar.

You can buy a single origin tonight. Or we can show up on Monday with inventory, a clean machine, and a standing order that does not forget itself.
